Studio is where you design, run and inspect your backends. It runs at port **8090** and is built with Sillo, `sillo-inertia` and React. It never touches a database directly: every screen calls the services' management APIs through Studio's bridge, so anything you do in Studio you can also automate with the [Platform API](/reference/environment-settings).

## Layout

- **Sidebar**: the environment switcher (project · environment) and every section, grouped into *Build*, *Automate*, *Services* and *Operate*.
- **Top bar**: breadcrumbs and the light/dark switch.
- **Open API docs**: the environment's generated reference, available to operators whether or not [public docs](/reference/environment-settings) are on.

## Editors are forms, JSON is one tab away

Every definition opens in a side panel with a real form: field lists with types and constraints, a **rule builder** for policy conditions, pickers filled with your actual policies, flows, functions, schemas and resources, cron presets, a mail preview. A **JSON** tab shows exactly what is stored and edits it directly; switching back rebuilds the form from your JSON.

## Operators

Studio users are operators, members of the reserved `_platform` project. Every management action they take lands in the [audit log](/operate/audit-log).
